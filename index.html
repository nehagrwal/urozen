Urozen – adverse effects and risks

The safety of Urozen depends on its exact ingredients, the doses used, the quality of manufacturing, and the user’s health and medication history. Botanical extracts can cause digestive upset or allergic reactions . Zinc can cause nausea and other gastrointestinal symptoms , especially at higher doses . L-arginine may lower blood pressure in some people so it’s important to watch out for potential interactions with blood pressure medications and other treatments.

Interactions are an important consideration for men who are taking prescription medications or who have ongoing health problems. Depending on the formulation, ingredients may interact with medicines that affect blood pressure, blood clotting or other physiological processes. A pharmacist or health care provider can look at the entire label to decide if a product is appropriate.

In the United States, unlike prescription drugs, dietary supplements do not have to be approved by the Food and Drug Administration for safety and effectiveness before they are marketed. Manufacturing and labelling requirements continue to apply, and the FDA has the authority to take action against products that violate applicable regulations. Consumers should seek clear information from manufacturers and, where available, credible independent quality testing. Severe difficulty urinating, blood in the urine, fever with urinary symptoms, or sudden inability to urinate requires urgent medical attention and not self-treatment with a supplement.
